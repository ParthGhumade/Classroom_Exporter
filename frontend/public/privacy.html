Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Classroom Exporter ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our service to export data from Google Classroom.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Classroom Exporter, we access the following information through Google's APIs:

  • Google Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture, used solely for authentication purposes.
  • Google Classroom Data: Course information, coursework, student submissions, and related materials that you choose to export.

We do not collect any information beyond what is necessary to provide the export functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and authorize access to your Google Classroom data.
  • Data Export: To retrieve and package your selected Classroom data for download.
  • Export History: To maintain a record of your export jobs so you can track their status.

4. Data Storage & Zero-Retention Architecture

Classroom Exporter is designed with a privacy-first, zero-storage architecture. We do not permanently store, collect, or mirror your Google Classroom course contents, documents, or student submissions on any intermediate server.

All file transfers occur directly between Google APIs and your authorized destination:

  • Export to Google Drive: Files are copied directly within Google's cloud infrastructure into your own Google Drive account.
  • ZIP Download: Files stream directly into your local browser's memory and disk. No file contents are stored or logged on our servers.

Export history logs are kept locally in your browser's private local storage (or your connected personal account) and can be cleared at any time.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data or Google Classroom contents with any third parties or advertising networks. We do not use your data for training machine learning or AI models.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Direct client-to-Google TLS/HTTPS encryption for all API calls.
  • OAuth 2.0 authorization with scoped tokens that never expose your Google account password.
  • Tokens are stored securely in browser session storage and cleared upon logout.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Classroom Exporter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your account and data:

  • You may revoke Classroom Exporter's permissions at any time via your Google Account Security settings.
  • You can delete your local export history at any time directly through the app interface or by clearing your browser cache.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
