Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Classroom Exporter ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our service to export data from Google Classroom.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Classroom Exporter, we access the following information through Google's APIs:

  • Google Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture, used solely for authentication purposes.
  • Google Classroom Data: Course information, coursework, student submissions, and related materials that you choose to export.

We do not collect any information beyond what is necessary to provide the export functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and authorize access to your Google Classroom data.
  • Data Export: To retrieve and package your selected Classroom data for download.
  • Export History: To maintain a record of your export jobs so you can track their status.

4. Data Storage & Retention

We store minimal metadata about your export jobs (such as export status and timestamps) in our database. Your actual Google Classroom content is processed temporarily during export and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Session data is stored securely and is automatically deleted when you log out or after your session expires.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or Google Classroom data with any third parties. Your data is used exclusively to provide you with the export service.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication with Google
  • Encrypted session management

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Classroom Exporter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account settings.
  • Request deletion of any stored data by contacting us.
  • Choose which courses and data to export.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through our website.
